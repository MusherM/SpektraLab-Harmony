# SpektraLab 1.3.0 release notes

Release date: November 13, 2026 (Beijing time). Previous release: **1.2.0**.

[中文](1.3.0.md)

- **Corrected print brightness:** Fixed excessive darkening or brightening when applying film to JPEG and other standard images, for more natural tonal results.
- **Consistent automatic exposure:** Standard images and RAW now share balanced metering, matching the original engine's exposure policy. Preview rendering and full-resolution saving use the same policy.
- **Refined film frames:** Redrawn perforations, edge codes, film lettering and textures improve frame details on landscape and portrait photos, including saved images.
