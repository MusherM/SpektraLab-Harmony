# SpektraLab 1.4.0 release notes

Release date: November 17, 2026 (Beijing time). Previous release: **1.3.0**.

[中文](1.4.0.md)

- **New home recipes:** Added coast, black-and-white riverside and forest castle prints, pairing Portra 160 × Supra Endura, HP5 Plus 400 × Multigrade IV RC and Ektar 100 × Endura Premier. Apply each pairing with “Print this look”.
- **Responsive home gallery:** Show one, two or three cards according to the available width, with navigation dots and the final position adjusted for browsing on foldables and wider windows.
- **More compact wide-screen cards:** Reduced spacing between the title, film and paper sections to display the complete material descriptions without vertical scrolling inside wide-screen cards. The single-card layout on regular phones stays the same.
