# SpektraLab 1.7.0 release notes

Previous release: **1.6.0**.

[中文](1.7.0.md)

- **Crop and rotate photos**. Open Crop from the photo preview to crop freely, lock the original aspect ratio, choose a common landscape or portrait ratio, or rotate left or right by 90°.
- **Save the edited composition**. Save a crop and rotation directly on standard photos, or use the same composition when exporting a darkroom print.
