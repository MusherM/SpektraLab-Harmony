# 1.8.0

- **Borders and live previews** — The new Borders tab sits between Paper and Adjust and previews 41 borders on the current photo, including every previously available style.
- **Border framing** — When a border crops the image, drag or resize the crop box at the opening's aspect ratio. Reselecting the same border restores its framing, and saved photos use the preview composition.
- **Long-press reordering** — Drag to reorder borders, films, and papers. Compact and expanded views share the same order, which persists after restart. No Border stays at the top.
